# Haystack

Haystack is a public Discord paper trading bot for learning, competition, and community fun.

Discord Market: https://discord.com/discovery/applications/1511935445385220167

Landing Page: https://tm44.link

Users can check market quotes, build simulated portfolios, make paper trades, view trade history, and compete on leaderboards — all with no real money, no broker connection, and no financial advice.

## Features

- Real market quote lookup
- Simulated paper trading
- Per-user paper portfolios
- Per-server portfolio separation
- Trade history
- Server leaderboards
- Auto-pricing for paper buys and sells
- Rate limiting for public use
- User data deletion command

## Commands

### Getting Started

- `/about` — What Haystack is
- `/help` — Show command list
- `/support` — Support and project links

### Market Data

- `/quote` — Get a market quote by ticker

### Paper Trading

- `/balance` — Show your paper cash balance
- `/paperbuy` — Buy shares in your paper portfolio
- `/papersell` — Sell shares from your paper portfolio
- `/portfolio` — Show your paper holdings
- `/trades` — Show recent paper trades
- `/leaderboard` — Show this server's paper trading leaderboard

### Data

- `/delete_my_data` — Delete your Haystack paper trading data for the current server

## Important Disclaimer

Haystack is paper trading only.

Haystack does not place real trades, does not connect to broker accounts, and does not provide financial advice. All trades, balances, portfolios, and leaderboard values are simulated.

Market data may be delayed, inaccurate, unavailable, or incomplete.
